FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about free cloud storage, backup and security with CloudGate.

How much free storage do I get?
Every CloudGate account starts with 100GB of free storage, with no credit card required.
Is CloudGate really free to use?
Yes. The 100GB free tier includes automatic backup, Vault Lock, document scanning and file sharing, with no hidden fees or trial period to worry about.
Which platforms are supported?
CloudGate works on Android, iPhone, and directly in your browser through the web app, keeping your files in sync across all three.
Can I use CloudGate on more than one device at once?
Yes. Sign in on your phone, tablet and the web with the same free account, and every backed-up file stays current across each device automatically.
Is my data secure?
Your data is encrypted in transit between your device and CloudGate, and you can request deletion of your data at any time.
How can I share files and folders?
Open any file or folder in the app and choose Share to send it securely to anyone, on or off CloudGate.
Can I restore deleted files?
CloudGate's one-tap restore lets you bring your backed-up files onto a new or reset Android device in a single tap.
