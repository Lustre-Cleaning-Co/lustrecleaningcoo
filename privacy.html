Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Lustre Cleaning Co. collects, holds, uses and discloses personal information, and how you can access, correct or ask questions about that information. It is written in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Last updated: 15 August 2026 Applies to: Lustre Cleaning Co., Perth WA

1. Introduction

Lustre Cleaning Co. (ABN 00 000 000 000, "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This policy applies to personal information collected through our website, WhatsApp, phone, email, and directly during the course of providing cleaning services across the Perth metropolitan region.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not provide personal information to us, though this may limit our ability to provide you with a quote or Service.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect only the personal information reasonably necessary to provide quotes, deliver cleaning services, and communicate with you. This typically includes:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and the suburb or address of the property to be cleaned.
  • Booking details: the service requested, property size, preferred dates, and any access instructions or special requests you provide.
  • Communication records: messages exchanged with us via WhatsApp, phone, email or the contact form on this website, kept for service quality and record keeping purposes.
  • Payment information: we do not directly store full card details. Payments are processed through third party payment facilities, and only transaction records such as amount and date are retained on our side.
  • Commercial account details: for business clients, this may include company name, ABN and billing contact information for invoicing purposes.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, such as health information, except where you voluntarily disclose it to us, for example to explain an access requirement or a reason a particular approach to cleaning is needed, in which case it is used only for that stated purpose.

3. How we collect information

We collect personal information directly from you when you:

  • Submit an enquiry or booking request through the contact form on this website.
  • Message us via WhatsApp or call our published phone number.
  • Email us directly.
  • Communicate with our team before, during or after a Service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information about you from third parties, except where a property manager, real estate agency or business you are associated with provides your details to us in the course of arranging a Service on your behalf, or where you have otherwise consented to this.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Prepare and provide quotes for cleaning services.
  • Confirm, schedule, and carry out bookings.
  • Communicate with you about a booking, including confirmations, reminders, and any changes.
  • Process payments and maintain records for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Respond to feedback, complaints, or requests made under our service guarantees.
  • Improve our services and internal scheduling based on booking history, where you are a recurring client.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision making that would have a legal or similarly significant effect on you. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Cookies and website data

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to support core functionality, such as remembering your progress through the pricing calculator on our pricing page, or to understand general, non identifying patterns in how the website is used, such as which pages are visited most often.

We do not use cookies for third party advertising or cross site tracking. Most browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through their settings, though doing so may affect how some parts of the website function.

7. Data storage and security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. This includes restricting access to personal information to those within our business who genuinely need it to carry out their role, and relying on reputable third party providers, such as Web3Forms, who maintain their own security standards for data in transit.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security, and any transmission of information to us is at your own risk.

8. How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including ongoing service delivery, accounting and taxation record keeping requirements under Australian law, which generally require financial records to be kept for five years, and to respond to any claims or disputes that may arise from a Service.

Where you have not engaged our services for an extended period and there is no ongoing legal or accounting reason to retain your information, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de identify it.

9. Your rights, access and correction

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, request access to it, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. To make such a request, contact us using the details in Section 11 below.

We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe, generally within thirty days. We may need to verify your identity before providing access to personal information, to protect your privacy and the accuracy of our records. In some limited circumstances permitted by law, we may not be able to provide full access, for example where doing so would unreasonably impact the privacy of another person, in which case we will explain our reasoning to you.

10. Making a complaint

If you believe we have handled your personal information in a way that breaches this policy or the Australian Privacy Principles, please contact us directly first using the details in Section 11, so we have the opportunity to investigate and resolve the matter.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), which oversees privacy compliance in Australia, at oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes to this policy and contact

11.1 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the tools we use, or legal requirements. The current version is always available on this page, along with the date it was last updated. We encourage you to review it periodically.

11.2 Contact us

For any question about this policy, or to make an access, correction or complaint request, contact us at lustrecleaningandco@gmail.com, by phone on 0400 000 000, or via WhatsApp using the link at the top of this page. Our related Terms and Conditions also apply whenever you book a service with us.
