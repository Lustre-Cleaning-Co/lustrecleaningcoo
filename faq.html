Bond cleans and guarantees

The question everyone actually wants an honest answer to before moving out.

If your property manager raises a genuine cleaning issue within 72 hours of your end of lease clean, we come back and fix it at no extra charge. It is a straightforward promise about the standard of the clean itself, and we take it seriously precisely because moving is stressful enough already.

We can honestly guarantee that the cleaning itself meets the standard your agent expects, and we will fix it if it does not. What we cannot control is the rest of the bond decision, things like wall damage, unpaid rent, or missing items are entirely separate from cleaning and sit outside what any cleaning company can promise, ours included.

Yes, carpet steam cleaning is included as standard in every end of lease clean, not tacked on as an extra you have to remember to ask for. It is one of the details agents check closely, so we build it into the job from the start rather than leaving it out and hoping nobody notices.

Honestly, most agents are picky, that is the whole point of an exit inspection. Our bond cleans are run against the same style of checklist most Perth property managers actually use, oven, skirting boards, window tracks and all, so a thorough inspection should turn up very little to complain about.

Yes, every cleaner completes a national police clearance and reference checks before they ever join the team, well before they are anywhere near your home or workplace. We would want the same for anyone coming into our own homes, so we hold that standard for everyone we send into yours.
