How we price

Why costs move, and how we keep them fair

Three things move a cleaning price more than anything else: how often you book, the condition of the property, and how hard it is to get to. A weekly or fortnightly clean is always priced lower per visit than a one off, because the team already knows the layout and the job never has time to build up into a bigger one. A first clean, an end of lease job, or a home that has gone months without attention takes longer and gets priced closer to a deep clean, even if you only asked for a regular one.

Access matters more than most people expect. External windows above ground level, multi storey homes, and properties with limited parking all add time that has to be reflected somewhere. We would rather tell you that upfront than pad the base rate for every customer to cover it. That is also why our figures are ranges rather than one flat number, a 2 bedroom unit with easy access and a 2 bedroom house with a long driveway and awkward bin access are not the same job, even on paper.

We benchmark our rates against the Perth market rather than picking numbers out of the air. Independent cleaners in the metro area typically sit between $35 and $60 an hour, agencies and franchises toward the top of that band. We price close to the middle of it, high enough to keep insured, police cleared, experienced people on the team, low enough that you are not paying for a call centre or a franchise fee on top of the clean itself.

What we will not do is quote low to win the booking and then find extra charges once we are on site. Every add on listed above is optional and disclosed before you confirm, and if a job turns out to need more time than expected, you get a call, not a surprise invoice. Payment is due on the day by bank transfer or card, and commercial contracts can move to monthly invoicing once we have worked together a few times. Regular cleans run without a lock in contract, pause or cancel with a bit of notice and nothing chases you for it.
