Residential cleaning

4 services

The services most Perth homes actually book: a rhythm of regular visits to keep a house liveable, a proper reset when it needs more than a wipe down, a bond clean when the lease is up, and a fast turnover for anyone renting their place out short term.

Regular house cleaning

Weekly · Fortnightly · Monthly

Think of it as the maintenance layer under a busy life, the visit that stops small mess from ever becoming a Saturday lost to it. The same team learns your home, works to the same checklist every time, and quietly keeps kitchens, bathrooms and living areas at a standard you don't have to think about between visits.

A typical visit covers benchtops and sinks degreased, bathrooms scrubbed and disinfected, floors vacuumed and mopped, surfaces dusted, and bins emptied. Bed changes, folding, and light tidying are available on request, so the service can flex around a household with kids, pets, or a home office that gets messy fast. Because the same cleaner tends to return, they start noticing the details that matter to your place specifically, the shelf that always collects dust, the rug that needs a firmer pass, without you having to point it out twice.

2 hour minimum visit No lock in contract Same cleaner where possible

End of lease and bond cleaning

Bond back guarantee

Moving is stressful enough without wondering if a real estate agent is going to nitpick the clean on the way out. This service is built specifically to satisfy that inspection: we work from the same style of checklist property managers use across Perth, so nothing gets missed on a technicality.

Every job includes carpet steam cleaning, oven and rangehood degreasing, inside of cupboards and wardrobes, skirting boards, light switches and door frames, plus windows, tracks and sills. It's the most thorough clean we offer, because it has to be. And because getting a bond back shouldn't come down to luck, every end of lease clean carries a 72 hour bond back guarantee: if your agent flags an issue in that window, we come back and fix it at no extra charge, full stop.

Carpet steam clean included 72 hour re-clean window Agent checklist standard

Deep and spring cleaning

One off reset

Every home builds up a layer of grime that a regular clean never quite reaches, behind the fridge, inside the oven, along the top of the skirting boards, in the corners a vacuum glides straight past. A deep clean is the corrective visit that goes after all of it in one pass, so whatever comes after, whether that's a regular schedule or nothing at all, starts from an actual clean baseline.

It's the service people book before hosting a big event, after finishing a renovation that's left dust everywhere, when moving into a new place before unpacking, or simply once or twice a year as proper home maintenance. Expect inside the oven and fridge, cupboard interiors emptied and wiped, skirting boards and window sills, and furniture pulled out and cleaned behind. It's slower and more hands on than a regular visit by design, this is the one time everything actually gets touched.

Inside oven, fridge, cupboards Skirting boards and sills Behind and under furniture

Airbnb and short stay turnovers

Fast turnaround

A short stay listing lives or dies on what the next guest sees in the first thirty seconds, and that window between checkout and check-in is rarely as wide as hosts would like. This service is built around that pressure: fast, consistent, and finished to a standard that photographs the way your listing promises it will.

Every turnover includes a full linen change and bed remake, kitchen and bathroom reset, surfaces wiped, rubbish taken out, and a final walk through against your own listing photos so nothing feels off to a returning guest. For hosts running a busy calendar, we can work to a recurring turnover schedule tied to your booking platform, which also brings the per clean rate down. Restocking of amenities like toiletries or coffee pods can be arranged too, just let us know what your listing includes.

Linen change and bed remake Recurring schedules available Photo ready finish

Commercial cleaning

Built around business hours rather than around us. Recurring contracts for offices, clinics and shared building spaces, run early or late so cleaning is never something clients or staff have to work around.

Office and commercial cleaning

After hours or early morning

An office that looks after itself is one clients and staff never have to think about, and that's the bar we work to. Scheduled cleans run before opening or after everyone's gone home, so the workday is never interrupted and nobody's stepping around a mop bucket mid-meeting.

The scope covers workstations and desks, kitchens and breakrooms, restrooms fully sanitised, and general common areas kept presentable, tailored to how your space is actually used rather than a generic checklist. Clinics, retail fitouts and hospitality venues each have different priorities, a reception area that needs to look immaculate at 9am, a treatment room that needs full sanitisation between clients, and we scope the visit around whichever of those matters most to your business. Larger contracts and multi-site accounts get a dedicated account setup and consistent invoicing, so cleaning becomes one less thing to manage rather than one more.

Scheduled around your hours Multi-site accounts available

Strata and common area cleaning

Fixed recurring roster

The lobby, stairwells and car park of a building say more about how it's managed than any individual apartment does, and they're the first thing every resident and visitor actually sees. This service keeps those shared spaces presentable on a schedule agreed directly with the strata manager or body corporate, not an afterthought squeezed in around other jobs.

Coverage typically includes lobby and entryway, stairwells and lift areas, car park sweeping, and shared facilities like gyms or common rooms where relevant. We work to a fixed roster so the committee always knows when to expect us, and we leave sign off sheets on site after every visit, giving an easy paper trail of what was done and when, useful at AGM time or whenever residents ask. Quoting is done against the building's own common property plan, so the roster reflects the actual size and complexity of the site rather than a flat rate that doesn't fit.

Agreed with strata manager Sign off sheets left on site

Specialist cleaning

4 services

Jobs that need specific equipment or a particular technique rather than a general clean, most often booked on their own or bundled in with a bond clean or deep clean for a better combined result.

Carpet and upholstery cleaning

Hot water extraction

Carpet holds onto dust, allergens and old stains in a way vacuuming alone never fully clears, which is why we use hot water extraction rather than a surface pass. Stains get pre-treated before the extraction step, lifting marks that have been sitting for months rather than just dampening them temporarily.

The process is safe for most common carpet fibres and upholstery fabrics found in Perth homes, and covers rugs, lounges and dining chairs as separate items when a full room treatment isn't what's needed. It's frequently booked alongside an end of lease clean, since fresh carpets can make the biggest visible difference to how a property presents at final inspection, and combining the two into one visit is usually more efficient for everyone.

Stain pre-treatment included Rugs and upholstery quoted separately

Window cleaning, in and out

Tracks and sills included

A window that's clean on one side but grimy on the other is somehow more noticeable than one that's dirty all over, so we always work both faces of the glass along with the tracks and sills that tend to get ignored. The goal is genuinely streak free glass, not the smeared, half done finish a rushed job leaves behind.

Homes and apartments are priced by time on site, while commercial shopfronts and multi-pane buildings are usually quoted per pane instead, since pane count and access matter more there than hours worked. Double storey homes and hard to reach windows are assessed either from photos you send through or during an in-person look, so the right access equipment and safety approach get factored in before we ever book the job in.

Tracks and sills wiped down Shopfronts quoted per pane

Oven cleaning

Food safe products

Baked on grease is one of the hardest things to shift in a kitchen and one of the first things a bond inspection checks, so it gets a dedicated process rather than a quick wipe. Racks are soaked and scrubbed separately, glass is degreased until it's actually clear again, and the interior is stripped back without leaving behind the harsh chemical smell some quick fixes do.

Everything used is food safe, which means the oven can go straight back into use the same day, no lingering fumes, no need to run it empty a few times first. Heavily soiled ovens, the kind that have had years of cooking build up without a proper clean, take extra time and a more intensive approach, but come out looking close to new either way. It's one of the most commonly added extras on a regular or deep clean booking, since it's easy to bundle in while a team is already on site.

Racks, glass and interior Usable again same day

Post construction cleaning

Multi-pass process

Construction dust behaves differently to household dust, it's finer, it settles slowly, and it resettles again after a single wipe down as if nothing happened. A newly built or renovated space genuinely needs multiple passes to come good, which is exactly why this is its own service rather than an extended deep clean.

We work through in stages: an initial rough clean to clear the bulk of debris and offcuts, then a detailed pass on surfaces, fittings and glass once the fine dust has had a chance to settle, and a final polish once everything else is done. Single zone jobs, a newly renovated kitchen or bathroom, are scoped differently to a full home handover after a build, and both are quoted around the specific trade work involved. This service covers interior spaces only, driveways, paths and exterior surfaces are handled as a separate quote since they usually need different equipment entirely.

Worked in multiple passes Interior spaces only
