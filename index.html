What we do

One team for every kind of clean.

Residential, commercial and specialist services, book them separately or bundle them into one visit. Full pricing for each is on our pricing page.

Residential

Regular house cleaning

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits from the same team working to the same checklist, covering kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, dusting, vacuuming and mopping.

Residential

End of lease and bond cleaning

A full clean run against your property manager's own checklist, with carpet steam cleaning included and a bond back guarantee behind it.

Residential

Deep and spring cleaning

A one off reset that goes further than a regular visit: inside the oven and fridge, cupboard interiors, skirting boards, window sills, and behind or under furniture.

Residential

Airbnb and short stay turnovers

Fast turnaround between guests with a consistent finish, including a linen change and bed remake, kitchen and bathroom reset, and rubbish out.

Commercial

Office and commercial cleaning

Scheduled cleans for offices, clinics, retail and hospitality venues, run after hours or before opening so the business day is never interrupted.

Commercial

Strata and common area cleaning

Recurring care for lobbies, stairwells, lift areas, car parks and shared facilities, worked to a fixed roster agreed with the strata manager.

Specialist

Carpet and upholstery cleaning

Hot water extraction with pre-treatment on stains, safe for most carpet fibres and upholstery fabrics, including rugs, lounges and dining chairs.

Specialist

Window cleaning, in and out

Streak free glass, tracks and sills for homes, apartments and shopfronts, with double storey and hard to reach windows quoted after a look at access.

Specialist

Oven cleaning

Racks, glass and interior degreased using food safe products, no harsh fumes left behind and the oven usable again the same day.

Specialist

Post construction clean

Fine dust, debris and residue removal in careful passes, so a freshly built or renovated space is genuinely ready to live in, not just swept.
